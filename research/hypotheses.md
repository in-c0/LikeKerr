# Research hypotheses

These are **assumed enough to build**, not treated as established findings.

## H1 — Effort reconstruction
Football locomotion data can be transformed into a useful player-effort rhythm.

## H2 — Physiological translation
A player's relative effort rhythm can be mapped to another athlete's personalised target HR trajectory.

## H3 — Closed-loop control
HR plus treadmill speed/incline can keep a user meaningfully synchronised with that target rhythm.

## H4 — Product experience
Following a real match-derived effort rhythm is useful and engaging enough to repeat.

## H5 — Immersion
A reconstructed VR/MR match materially improves training experience without unacceptable balance or gait cost.

## H6 — Directional fidelity
Adding directional locomotion materially improves training value enough to justify specialised hardware.

## H7 — Sports ODT
A sports-grade omnidirectional treadmill can support athletic multidirectional locomotion with acceptable safety and biomechanics.

## Research-in-public rule

For each experiment publish:
1. belief / hypothesis;
2. why it is plausible;
3. implementation;
4. measurement;
5. predeclared success criterion where practical;
6. result;
7. what changed.

Do not publish enabling details of potentially patentable hardware/control inventions until the IP decision is made.
